Our story

Aspire Home Audio Plus was founded in 2011 out of a real love for sound and technology. Since then we have designed and installed smart home, home theater and audio systems in private houses, penthouses and luxury apartments in Jerusalem and across Israel.

We are Israel's leading Vitrea dealer an authorized Control4 dealer and the 2026 Control4 Partner of the Year award winner. Above all, though, we are field people: every project starts with a home visit, continues with precise planning, and ends only when everything works at a single touch.
